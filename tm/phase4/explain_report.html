HDC–TM authorship verifier — forensic report

Margin = Σ(positive clause votes) − Σ(negative clause votes), averaged over 3 independently seeded ensemble members. Positive favours same author. Attributions below are occlusion measurements: a word type is deleted from both documents, both are re-encoded, and the change in margin is recorded. Occlusion is used because an HDC dimension is a superposition of all features — per-dimension “decoding” would be ill-posed, aggregate deletion is not.

How to read this — and its limits

  • Exact where it claims to be. The margin is literally the clause-vote sum; clause literals are literally thermometer bands of specific sketch dimensions. No surrogate model stands between the report and the decision.
  • Attributions are aggregate, not per-dimension. Each number is a measured deletion effect on the whole pipeline. Individual HDC dimensions carry no isolated meaning (superposition), so they are deliberately not reported.
  • Thermometer coding makes effects piecewise. A small change can be invisible until it crosses a band edge; attributions are therefore not smooth derivatives.
  • Ensemble disagreement is diagnostic. Training is stochastic (unseeded shuffle + HogWild threading cause documented AUC swings); cases where members split on direction should be treated as low-confidence regardless of the averaged margin.
  • The margin is not a likelihood ratio. For forensic reporting it must pass through the calibrator (and, in the deployed stack, fusion with KN λG) before being expressed as an LR.
