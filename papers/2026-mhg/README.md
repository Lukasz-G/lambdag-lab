# DHd 2026 — Middle High German paper (backfill in progress)

Reproduction kit for "Stilometrie mit LambdaG für die mittelhochdeutsche
Literatur". The curated pipelines (ReM-based masking via
`POSNoiseMasker.pretagged("gmh")`, fragment protocols, calibration) will be
backfilled here; the gmh/gml pattern-list builders already live in `tools/`
and the lists in `posnoise_lists/` (see `docs/posnoise_lists.md` for their
provenance and caveats).

Corpus note: ReM/ReN cannot be redistributed here — fetch them from their
official sources; the scripts consume the corpora's own tab format.
